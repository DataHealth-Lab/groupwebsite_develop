## Profile  
PhD candidate in the Graduate Program in Infectious and Parasitic Diseases at UFMS. Holds a Master's degree in Infectious and Parasitic Diseases from UFMS. Specialist in Combating COVID-19 and other viral diseases, having completed the Educational Program on Health Surveillance and Care for Combating COVID-19 and other viral diseases (VigiEpidemia) at Fiocruz Mato Grosso do Sul. Holds a degree in Nursing from the University of Mato Grosso do Sul (Campo Grande Campus). She have been a scholarship recipient under the CNPq/PIBIC Institutional Program for Scientific and Technological Initiation Scholarships during the 2019-2020 and 2020-2021 cycles, focusing on epidemiology; during this time, she conducted research on "Epidemiology of pregnant women with Zika virus infection in Campo Grande, Mato Grosso do Sul, 2015-2017" and ["Clinical characterization of live births exposed to the Zika virus in Campo Grande, Mato Grosso do Sul, 2015-2017"](https://www.scielo.br/j/rsbmt/a/HtPcP4YjbthYNXmYk4Czj6N/?lang=en), respectively. During her master's studies, she investigated [vaccination coverage](https://www.scielo.br/j/csp/a/d8KrYbgnfrwmHGrf9ntMf6h/?lang=en) among children in the city of Campo Grande, MS.
