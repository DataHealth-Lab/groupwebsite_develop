I am a health information management professional and research coordinator based in Nairobi, Kenya. I am the Project Coordinator for the Kenya Critical Care Registry (KCCR) under the Critical Care Society of Kenya, where I oversee registry operations, data quality, ethics coordination, and the use of critical care data to improve patient outcomes. I also coordinate multicountry critical care research, including the EXTREME-ICU study. I hold an MSc in Data Science with Commendation from the University of Aberdeen. My thesis used distributed lag non-linear models (DLNM) to look at the link between temperature and ICU admissions in Kenya. I am interested in using health data, data science, and digital health to strengthen health systems and support evidence-based decisions, especially in resource-limited settings.
