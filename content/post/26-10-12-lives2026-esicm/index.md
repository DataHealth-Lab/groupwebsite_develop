From 10 to 14 October 2026, Lisbon hosted **LIVES 2026**, the 39th Annual Congress of the European Society of Intensive Care Medicine ([ESICM](https://www.esicm.org/)), one of the largest annual gatherings in critical care research and clinical practice. The DataHealth Lab was there in full: debating on stage, moderating poster sessions, and presenting three posters across the week.

### ⭐ Otavio as Rising Star

[Otavio](https://datahealthlab.org/author/otavio-ranzani/) was selected as a **Rising Star** by ESICM, a designation given to emerging leaders in intensive care medicine who take on featured roles across the scientific programme: from debates to moderation of abstract sessions.

{{< figure src="risingstar_lives26.png" title="Otavio listed as Rising Star in the LIVES 2026 official programme" lightbox="true" width="400">}}

### 🎤 Debate

On Monday 12 October, Otavio participated as a debater in the session *From infection to sepsis: how to identify patients at risk of a dysregulated host response*, alongside Massimo Girardis (Modena, Italy) and Evangelos J. Giamarellos-Bourboulis (Athens, Greece), bringing a data-driven perspective to one of critical care's most pressing questions in precise medicine in sepsis.

{{< figure src="debate_lives2026.jpg" title="Otavio at the debate session, LIVES 2026" lightbox="true" width="400">}}

### 🗂️ Moderating poster sessions

As part of the Rising Star programme, Otavio co-moderated two abstract sessions: **Infection** (Tuesday 13 October, with Anahita Rouzé from Lille, France) and **Systemic Inflammation and Sepsis** (Wednesday 14 October, with J. Pedro Teixeira from Albuquerque, USA).

### 📋 Posters

All three posters are published in the [ESICM LIVES 2026 abstract supplement](https://link.springer.com/journal/40635/volumes-and-issues/14-1/supplement) of *Intensive Care Medicine Experimental*.

**[Mario Zaccarelli](https://datahealthlab.org/author/mario-zaccarelli/)** presented *Mechanical Ventilation Weaning Outcomes in Neurocritical vs Non-Neurocritical Patients: A Post-Hoc Analysis of Two Multicenter Randomized Controlled Trials* ([abstract](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40635-026-00962-w)).

{{< figure src="mario_poster_lives2026.jpg" title="Mario Zaccarelli presenting at LIVES 2026" lightbox="true" width="400">}}

**Dr. Carles Subirà**, chief of clinical research at the ICU of the Hospital de la Santa Creu i Sant Pau and lead of the [Critical Care group at IRSANTPAU](https://www.recercasantpau.cat/en/w/medicina-intensiva), presented *Predicting risk of reintubation after successful spontaneous breathing trial: a post hoc analysis of two randomized clinical trials* ([abstract](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40635-026-00962-w)); work developed in collaboration with our lab.

{{< figure src="carles_poster_lives2026.jpg" title="Dr. Carles Subirà presenting at LIVES 2026" lightbox="true" width="300">}}

**Dr. Nora Fuentes**, from Prof. Mariano Esperatti's group at Hospital Privado de Comunidad (Mar del Plata, Argentina), presented *Frequency and determinants of awake prone positioning in intensive care units* — a multinational cross-sectional study on awake prone positioning in acute respiratory failure, developed in collaboration with our lab ([abstract](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40635-026-00962-w)).

### 🤝 LOGIC meeting

The congress also brought together the [LOGIC network](https://icubenchmarking.com/), an international collaborative consortia of ICU registries, which held its meeting during LIVES 2026.

Congratulations to everyone involved! 👏
