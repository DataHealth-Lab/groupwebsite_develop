🌍 On 29 and 30 October 2026, the **30th International Symposium on Infections in the Critically Ill Patient and International Sepsis Forum ([ISICIP-ISF](https://www.isicip.com/))** took place in Barcelona, at the Faculty of Medicine of the University of Barcelona. This anniversary edition was organised together with the **International Sepsis Forum (ISF)** under the name *Sepsis 2026*, and brought together clinicians and researchers in critical care and infectious diseases, with a strongly interactive format.

### 🎤 Lecture

[Otavio Ranzani](https://datahealthlab.org/author/otavio-ranzani/) gave the lecture *Global Sepsis Epidemiology in the Big Data Era* on Thursday 29 October, in the session *AI and the Digital ICU*. The talk reviewed earlier and recent data on sepsis around the world and presented unpublished results from our group.

### 🗂️ Moderating and chairing

- **Organ Crosstalk and Kidney Injury:** Otavio co-moderated this session on Thursday 29 October with Prof. Marc Leone (Marseille). It covered sepsis-associated acute kidney injury, including its mechanisms and the crosstalk between the kidney and the immune system.
- **Best abstracts:** on Friday 30 October, Otavio chaired the presentation of the best abstracts in the main room, with Silvia Da Rosa (Trento).
